# Code of Conduct

Tide Flow VN is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free collaboration space.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas, code, data, and evidence—not people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, licenses, and the right of maintainers to keep changes focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, spam, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces when someone is representing the project.
